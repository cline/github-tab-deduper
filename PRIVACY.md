# Privacy Policy — GitHub Tab De-duper

**GitHub Tab De-duper does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any user
data.**

## What the extension can see

To detect duplicates, the extension reads the URLs of open `github.com`
tabs. These URLs are compared in memory and immediately discarded — they
are never recorded, stored, or transmitted anywhere. The extension cannot
see tabs on any other site, injects no scripts into web pages, and makes
no network requests of any kind.

## What the extension stores

- **Your settings** — three on/off preferences, saved via Chrome's
  `storage.sync` so they follow your Chrome profile.
- **A counter** — a single number tracking how many duplicate tabs have
  been closed, saved locally on your device and shown in the popup.

That is the complete list. Both are deleted by Chrome when you uninstall
the extension.

## What the extension sends over the network

Nothing. The extension contains no analytics, no telemetry, no error
reporting, and no remote code.
